Privacy notice

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This calendar planner and the Career Connect guide and workshop catalog are provided by Year Up United to help you plan and attend your Career Connect workshops. This notice explains what these pages do and do not collect.

What stays in your browser

The calendar planner runs entirely in your browser. The workshops and session times you select are used only to create your calendar file (the “.ics” download and add-to-calendar links). They are not saved and not sent anywhere. These pages do not ask for or store your name, email, or any other personal details.

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how students use these pages and make them easier to use, we use Microsoft Clarity, an analytics service. Clarity collects anonymous information about how the page is used — pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, and general device and browser type — and may record an anonymized playback of a visit. On-screen content is masked so that anything you type is not captured. Clarity also uses cookies to recognize a visit as it moves across these pages.

This information is used only in aggregate to improve the Career Connect experience. It is not used to identify you and is not sold. Analytics data is processed by Microsoft under Microsoft’s privacy terms: Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Registration and attendance are handled in the student portal, not on these pages. Anything related to your enrollment, registration, or attendance is governed by Year Up United’s own privacy practices. [YUU: link your main privacy policy here if you have one.]
