Your 6-week journey

How workshops work

Every week follows the same simple rhythm. Here's what to expect.

1

2 workshop topics weekly

Each week has two live workshop topics, with several session times to choose from.

2

Monday–Thursday options

Workshops are offered Monday through Thursday so you can register for sessions that fit your week.

3

2 PM, 6 PM, or 8 PM ET

Most sessions are offered at these times. Pick the time you can attend live.

4

Hands-on career practice

Sessions focus on practical skills for confidence, resumes, interviews, LinkedIn, AI, and workplace situations.
