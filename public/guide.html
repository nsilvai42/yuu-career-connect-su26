Make it stick

Set yourself up for success

1
Add sessions to your calendar
Once you’ve registered, use the calendar tool to add every session — blocking the time makes you far more likely to show up.
2
Post your plan on Slack
Tell your cohort which workshops you picked — a public plan makes it easier to follow through.
3
Switch slots — don’t skip
Life gets busy. If something comes up, reschedule to another session — a switch beats a miss.
4
Front-load Career Essentials (CORE)
Aim for all 12, but if you are short on time, complete the orange-marked CORE workshops first.
